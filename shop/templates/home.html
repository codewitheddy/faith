Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering from The POPSHOP.KE

How do I place an order? +

Simply browse our collection, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You'll be redirected to WhatsApp where you can confirm your order details, provide your delivery address, and complete your purchase with our team.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept M-Pesa, bank transfers, and cash on delivery. Payment details will be shared via WhatsApp after you place your order. We'll guide you through the entire payment process.

How long does delivery take? +

Delivery within Nairobi takes 1-2 business days. For other locations in Kenya, delivery takes 2-4 business days. We'll provide tracking information once your order is dispatched.

Do you offer free delivery? +

Yes! We offer free delivery within Nairobi for orders above Ksh 2,000. For orders below this amount or outside Nairobi, a small delivery fee applies based on your location.

Can I return or exchange items? +

Yes, we accept returns and exchanges within 7 days of delivery. Items must be in original condition with tags attached. Contact us via WhatsApp to initiate a return or exchange.

Are your jewellery pieces authentic? +

Absolutely! We source all our jewellery from trusted suppliers and ensure quality checks before listing. Each piece is carefully selected for its craftsmanship and beauty.

Do you offer gift wrapping? +

Yes! We offer complimentary gift wrapping for all orders. Just mention it in the notes section during checkout, and we'll beautifully package your jewellery.

How do I care for my jewellery? +

Store your jewellery in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Clean gently with a soft cloth. Avoid contact with perfumes, lotions, and water to maintain shine and longevity.
