## Quick Customization

### Add More Testimonials
Copy this block in `home.html`:
```html
<div class="testimonial-card">
    <div class="testimonial-quote">"</div>
    <div class="testimonial-stars">★★★★★</div>
    <p class="testimonial-text">Review text...</p>
    <div class="testimonial-author">
        <div class="testimonial-avatar">X</div>
        <div class="testimonial-info">
            <div class="testimonial-name">Name</div>
            <div class="testimonial-location">City</div>
        </div>
    </div>
</div>
```

### Update Social Links
Replace URLs in footer with your actual handles:
- Instagram: `https://www.instagram.com/YOUR_HANDLE`
- Facebook: `https://www.facebook.com/YOUR_PAGE`
- Twitter: `https://twitter.com/YOUR_HANDLE`
- LinkedIn: `https://www.linkedin.com/company/YOUR_COMPANY`
